Explore a landscape search.

A Swiss roadside scene illustrates what a broad location search looks like beyond a city centre.

WhereWas field notes · Illustrative example · 3 min read

Why a shortlist?

Not every place photo shows a recognisable building. Landscapes and rural roads can also prompt a location search, but several areas may look alike. An honest shortlist helps you explore possibilities.

Review the result

Compare candidates on the map and pay attention to the radius. When the result covers a wider area, more decimal places in its centre coordinate do not make the estimate precise.

Keep the context

WhereWas keeps the source photo, suggested places and map in one view. You can inspect the surrounding area and decide whether the result is useful for your purpose.
