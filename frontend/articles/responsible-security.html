FIELD NOTE / 03 / UNITED KINGDOM · LONDON

Compare places responsibly.

A London street scene shows why a shortlist and a visible radius are useful when places look alike.

WhereWas field notes · Illustrative example · 3 min read

Why a shortlist?

Similar buildings and streets can appear in several places. A location tool is most useful when it offers candidates you can inspect, with enough context to understand the limits of each suggestion.

Review the result

WhereWas keeps the photo and map together. Review nearby areas and compare candidates before making a decision that depends on the location. Do not treat the centre of a search radius as an exact capture point.

Keep the context

WhereWas is designed for photos of places. When a person is prominent, the result is deliberately limited to a broad city area. That keeps the workflow focused on places while allowing ordinary street scenes with incidental passersby.

Location estimate, not a certain address.

Scores order candidates for review. Use the map and radius when assessing a result.
