Field note / responsible use

Building a visual investigation workflow people can trust.

Visual location tools are most valuable when they help authorised teams inspect evidence—not when they hide uncertainty or encourage misuse.

Illustrative field note · 5 min read

Security and research teams often receive imagery with incomplete context: a site photograph, an incident update, a field report, or an insurance attachment. Visual location verification can help create candidates for review, but it should not become a shortcut around lawful process.

Design for authorised work

Organisation accounts, role-based access, case identifiers, retention controls, and audit logs make a workflow clearer for legitimate use. They also set an important boundary against covert tracking or identifying private people.

Useful location intelligence is inspectable, proportionate, and tied to an authorised purpose.

Keep people in the loop

The reviewer should see the source image, the clues, the ranked locations, and the confidence before acting. A tool can accelerate investigation without pretending to replace judgment.

WhereWas field notes are illustrative product content. The product should never claim certainty where the image cannot support it.
