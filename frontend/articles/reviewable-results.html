Explore a city search result.

A Mumbai place photo demonstrates how to compare candidates and keep the search radius visible.

WhereWas field notes · Illustrative example · 3 min read

Why a shortlist?

City streets can look similar, and a familiar looking result can still be wrong. WhereWas gives you more than one possible place to inspect instead of asking you to accept a single location.

Review the result

Start with the ranked list, then open the candidates on the map. The score orders suggestions for review; it is not a measured probability that a specific address is correct. The radius shows how much area the estimate covers.

Keep the context

Street context is useful for exploring the surroundings of a suggested place. Nearby imagery may differ in date or camera direction, so keep your own judgement in the loop.

Location estimate, not a certain address.

Scores order candidates for review. Use the map and radius when assessing a result.
