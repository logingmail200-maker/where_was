FIELD NOTE / 01 / INDIA · BENGALURU

Review a location shortlist.

An everyday Bengaluru street is a useful example of why a location result should be explored on a map, not accepted as a pin.

WhereWas field notes · Illustrative example · 3 min read

Why a shortlist?

The picture shows an ordinary street rather than a famous landmark. That is precisely the sort of place photo people want help locating. WhereWas presents possible places as a shortlist, making it easier to compare them without treating the first suggestion as certain.

Review the result

Open a candidate on the map and consider its search radius. A broad radius means the result covers an area, even when the map shows a precise centre point. The result should remain a lead until you have reviewed the surroundings.

Keep the context

The workflow keeps the uploaded photo, candidate list, map and available street context together. You can move between suggestions and choose the one that best fits what you know about the image.

Location estimate, not a certain address.

Scores order candidates for review. Use the map and radius when assessing a result.
